Evolving threats are the top challenge to operational resilience
Data theft and system lockouts are still hot commodities for bad actors. However, they aren’t stopping there. They’re increasingly turning to extortion, psychological intimidation, and physical threats to force payment.
“The bad guys are always evolving their tactics,” says Semperis CEO Mickey Bresman. “It makes sense that we'll continue to see attackers trying to find more ways to make victims pay.”

Holding you at physical risk is the latest phenomenon because we've covered most of the other risks.

Ransomware is damaging 
more than business
Revenue losses from business disruption, insurance hikes, and data theft are nothing new. But attackers aren’t stopping there. They are expanding their threats to the family and friends of their targets’ employees and leveraging tactics like doxxing, swatting, stalking, and even abduction. 
“Attackers play as dirty as they need to get what they want,” says Cyber Resiliency and Human Defense expert Sarah Gosler. “That’s why they're taking it beyond just a company's operation and making it personal. We need to start thinking about cyber attackers as the criminals that they are.”
