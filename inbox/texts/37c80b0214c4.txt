Notice Regarding Unauthorized System Access and Impact on Payment Services
We have confirmed that, beginning on 13 October, 2026, an external third party gained unauthorized access and logged into our systems. As a result, we have confirmed a data breach involving certain merchant information, and there is a possibility that personal data may have also been compromised.
Furthermore, our subsequent investigation confirmed unauthorized access to the CRM system used in our business operations. We are currently conducting a thorough investigation to determine the full scope of the impact.
In the process of responding to this incident, temporary disruptions occurred across a portion of our payment services.
We sincerely apologize for the considerable concern and inconvenience this incident has caused to all affected parties and stakeholders.
For additional details, please refer to the report attached below.
