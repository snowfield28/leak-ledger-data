CDJapan : Notice Regarding the Potential Leakage of Personal Information (Initial Report)
November 13, 2026
Neowing Corporation
Fumio Katagiri
President and Representative Director
Notice Regarding the Potential Leakage of Personal Information (Initial Report)
Neowing Corporation (headquartered in Nihonbashi, Tokyo; President: Fumio Katagiri; hereinafter referred to as "the Company") has confirmed that customers' personal information may have been exposed to unauthorized third parties as a result of unauthorized access to CDJapan and Neowing, the online shopping websites operated by the Company.
We sincerely apologize for the significant concern and inconvenience this situation has caused our customers.
We are currently investigating the cause of the incident and the extent of its impact. Below, we provide the information confirmed as of this date, along with important precautions for our customers.
1. Details of the Incident
On November 11, 2026, we confirmed that an issue with a web page had allowed unauthorized external access to Neowing System (our internal system), resulting in data tampering that may have exposed customers' credit card information to third parties.
We immediately blocked the affected access route and implemented measures to prevent further damage. We are continuing our investigation in cooperation with an independent cybersecurity investigation firm.
2. Information Potentially Affected
Customers Who May Be Affected
Customers who used CDJapan between November 9, 2026, at 12:00 PM and November 11, 2026, at 5:00 PM (JST).
Number of potentially affected cases: 285
Information That May Have Been Exposed
Credit card information newly registered or updated during the above period, including:
Credit card numbers
Expiration dates
Cardholder names
Security codes (CVV/CVC)
At this time, no actual leakage of personal information or secondary damage, such as unauthorized use, has been confirmed.
3. Actions Taken and Ongoing Measures
We are taking the following actions:
Investigation: Investigating the cause of the incident and the extent of its impact in cooperation with an independent cybersecurity investigation firm.
Regulatory notification: Submitted an initial report to Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission on November 13, 2026.
Police notification: Reported the incident to the National Police Agency's cybercrime reporting desk.
Customer notification: Preparing to contact potentially affected customers individually.
Credit card payments: As a precautionary measure, we have temporarily suspended credit card payments to ensure customer safety and prevent further potential risks.
4. Important Precautions for Customers
We kindly ask our customers to take the following precautions:
Beware of suspicious emails and SMS messages.
 Do not click on links in emails or text messages that you do not recognize. We will never ask you to provide your password or credit card information via email or SMS.
Review your passwords.
 If you use the same password for other online services, we recommend changing those passwords.
Check your credit card statements.
 Please review your credit card transactions for any unfamiliar or unauthorized charges. If you notice any suspicious transactions, contact your credit card issuer immediately.
5. Next Steps
We will provide further updates on our website as soon as new information becomes available through our ongoing investigation.
Customers who may have been affected will be contacted individually by email or other appropriate means.
6. Fulfillment of Existing Orders
We will continue to take full responsibility for fulfilling and delivering all orders that have already been placed.
7. Contact Information
For inquiries regarding the potential leakage of personal information, please contact CDJapan Customer Support.
Email: info@cdjapan.co.jp
Once again, we sincerely apologize for the significant inconvenience and concern this incident has caused our customers.
