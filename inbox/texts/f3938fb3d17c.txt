Notice Regarding the Temporary Closure of our Inquiry Form
2026 Oct. 16
Company Notice
News & Events
Astemo has issued the following announcement today.
Unauthorized access to a data-sharing cloud environment used by Astemo and resulting data breach 
Due to this incident, we have temporarily suspended all external inquiry forms--including the aftermarket business inquiry form--starting this week. 
We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and thank you for your understanding. 
Please note that there has been no data breach affecting our customers as a result of this unauthorized access to our cloud system. 
We will issue a further announcement as soon as we have a clear timeline for the reopening of the inquiry form. We would therefore ask for your patience in the meantime.
