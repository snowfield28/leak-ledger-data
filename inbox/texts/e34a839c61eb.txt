We believe the attack vector was contained within an hour of the first alert. Our investigation shows no sign that the attacker was in our environment after the attack.
Independent forensic auditors began reviewing the attack together with Northflank over the weekend, and they continue to review it.
Impact on your workloads
Running workloads were unaffected. At this time we have seen no sign that workloads or workload data were accessed during the attack. Evidence suggests it was focused on secrets held in our control plane. Customers on forward-deployed or dedicated control planes are unaffected.
Separately, we have patched CVE-2026-98432 across our fleet, including BYOC clusters where we have an active integration. If your BYOC cluster is no longer connected to Northflank, please contact support.
Over the last few days there has been intermittent platform degradation while we rotated our own keys and hardened the platform.
What you need to do
Some customers have reported unauthorised use of AI secrets. Please rotate all secrets now, both integration secrets and project secrets, even if you haven’t seen suspicious activity. We recommend starting with AI provider keys, cloud provider credentials, payment provider keys, private keys, and database and other service credentials.
We also recommend reviewing your provider logs for unusual activity. If you have seen unexpected AI usage or spend, contact your AI providers directly, such as OpenAI and Anthropic.
Where we were able to act on your behalf, we have revoked Northflank access tokens and sessions, VCS integrations, and cross-account IAM roles and service accounts. If you haven’t already, sign in again and reconnect your integrations.
Need help?
If you need help rotating secrets or checking that everything has been rotated, contact 
support@northflank.com
, or use your direct channel with us if you have one. We are treating this with the utmost urgency, and the entire organisation has been working 24/7.
What’s next
We will keep updating this page as the review progresses, and we intend to share a root cause analysis once we have confirmed findings.
