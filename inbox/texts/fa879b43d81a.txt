What It Means to Be an Academic Medical Center
The UNM Health Sciences Center is New Mexico’s only academic medical center and home to the state’s largest teaching hospital. Academic medical centers play a vital role in advancing healthcare by providing exceptional patient care, training the next generation of health professionals, and driving research that leads to innovative treatments and cures.
